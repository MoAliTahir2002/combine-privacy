Combine.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Combine. is made by Ali Tahir (“I”, “me”). This policy explains what the app does with your information.

The short version: your health data stays on your phone.

What Combine reads

With your permission, Combine reads the following from Apple Health (iPhone) or Health Connect (Android):

  • Active and resting calories burned, and total calories burned
  • Steps
  • Food logged: calories, protein, carbohydrates and fat
  • Weight, body fat percentage, lean body mass and BMI
  • Distance, floors climbed and exercise minutes
  • Heart rate (resting, average, maximum), heart-rate variability, VO₂ max, blood oxygen and breathing rate
  • Sleep duration, sleep stages and sleep times (also used to work out your usual bedtime for the nightly report)
  • Water, fibre, sugar and sodium logged

If you choose to, Combine also reads:

  • Hevy workouts via Hevy’s API, using an API key you provide, or from a CSV file you export from Hevy
  • Renpho body-composition readings from a CSV file you export from Renpho Health
  • Garmin Body Battery, stress and sleep score from a Garmin data-export file you choose

Combine never writes to Apple Health or Health Connect. On Android, if you allow background access, Combine also reads this data while the app is closed, only to keep your nightly report up to date.

Where your data goes

  • All of the data above is processed and stored only on your device. I do not run a server, and Combine does not upload your health data anywhere.
  • Your Hevy API key is stored in your phone’s secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore) and is only ever sent to Hevy (api.hevyapp.com) to download your workouts.
  • Combine has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Health data is never sold, shared, or used for advertising or marketing.
  • Purchases: if you buy Combine Premium, the payment is handled by Apple or Google. Combine uses a service called RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app ID and your purchase history for Combine (what was bought and when). It does not receive your name, your payment details or any health data. See revenuecat.com/privacy.
  • Notifications are created on your phone. Their content is not sent to me.
  • Coach reports: if you use “Send to coach”, Combine creates a PDF of your report on your phone and opens Messages (or your share sheet) with it attached. It is only sent if you press Send, and only to the person you choose.

Your choices

  • You can change which data Combine can read at any time in the Health app (iPhone: Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Combine) or in Health Connect (Android).
  • Settings → “Delete all Combine data” removes everything Combine has stored, including your Hevy key. Uninstalling the app does the same. Subscriptions are managed and cancelled in your App Store or Google Play account settings.

Children

Combine is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
